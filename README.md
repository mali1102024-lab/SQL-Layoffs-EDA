**Layoffs SQL Exploratory Data Analysis Project**

**Project Overview**

**Project Title:** Layoffs Exploratory Data Analysis

**Level:** Beginner–Intermediate

**Database:** layoffs_staging2

This project is part of the Data Analyst Bootcamp playlist and focuses on exploring and analyzing a layoffs dataset using SQL. The goal is to demonstrate SQL skills in performing exploratory data analysis (EDA) — identifying trends, distributions, and insights from the dataset. It mirrors real-world analyst tasks where cleaned data is further explored to answer business questions.

**Objectives**

**Data Exploration:** Understand the structure and contents of the layoffs dataset.

**Trend Analysis:** Explore layoffs by year, industry, and company.

**Distribution Checks:** Identify patterns across countries, industries, and stages.

**Business Insights:** Derive meaningful conclusions from the dataset to support decision-making.

**Project Structure**

**Dataset Overview**

Reviewed columns such as company, location, industry, total layoffs, percentage laid off, funds raised, and date.

**Exploratory Analysis**

Identified maximum layoffs and companies with complete workforce reductions.
Analyzed layoffs by company, country, and stage.
Summarized layoffs by year and month.
Built rolling totals to observe cumulative layoffs over time.
Ranked top 5 companies per year based on layoffs.
Insights Development
Highlighted industries and companies most affected.
Observed yearly and monthly trends in layoffs.
Compared layoffs across countries and company stages.
Derived averages for percentage laid off by company.

**Findings**

**Industry Impact:** Technology and crypto-related companies showed higher layoffs compared to other industries.

**Yearly Trends:** Layoffs peaked during specific years, reflecting global economic conditions.

**Company Insights:** A few companies accounted for a significant share of total layoffs.

**Geographic Distribution:** Layoffs varied across countries, with some regions more affected.

**Rolling Totals:** Cumulative layoffs revealed the scale of workforce reductions over time.

**Reports**

**Yearly Layoffs Report:** Layoffs grouped by year.

**Industry Analysis Report:** Layoffs summarized by industry.

**Top Companies Report:** Companies with the highest layoffs.

**Country Distribution Report:** Layoffs by geography.

**Rolling Totals Report:** Cumulative layoffs across months.

**Conclusion**

This project highlights exploratory data analysis in SQL, a critical skill for analysts. By examining layoffs across industries, companies, and years, the dataset provides valuable insights into workforce trends and economic shifts.
